ABOUT

Mission overview

IBVAP is an AI-powered video analytics prototype. It processes surveillance footage, detects people with a pretrained YOLO model, flags intrusions inside a defined restricted zone and creates SHA-256 fingerprints so output evidence can be checked for tampering.

FEATURES

Capabilities in this prototype

01PERSON DETECTION

Pretrained YOLO finds people in thermal and normal video and draws labelled boxes.

02RESTRICTED ZONE

A configurable zone is overlaid on the frame; people entering it become intruders.

03LIVE ALERT BANNER

Red perimeter-breach banner and intrusion counter update as events occur.

04EVIDENCE INTEGRITY

SHA-256 hash of the output file lets anyone verify it was not modified.

SCOPE

Limitations

This site presents the project overview. The prototype uses pretrained YOLO person detection, OpenCV processing, a sample rectangular zone and local SHA-256 hashing. RGB-thermal fusion, custom model training, digital signatures and blockchain are not claimed as completed features.
